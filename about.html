Who I Am

Hello, I’m Nirban Karmakar

I’m the founder of United Photography, based in Kolkata, India. Since 2015, I’ve had the privilege of documenting weddings alongside my team, capturing the stories, emotions, and beautiful chaos that make each celebration truly unique. Over the years, we’ve had the opportunity to photograph more than 300+ weddings, each one leaving us with memories of our own.

Our approach to wedding photography is simple, be present, but never intrusive.

We believe the most beautiful photographs are often the ones that aren’t planned. A quiet glance between two people, a parent holding back tears, a burst of laughter among friends, a nervous smile before the ceremony, or a fleeting moment that lasts only a second, these are the moments we look for.

With our fly-on-the-wall approach, we strive to capture your wedding as it naturally unfolds. Rather than directing every moment, we observe, anticipate, and document the emotions as they happen. Our aim is to tell a wedding story that captures not only the grandeur and larger-than-life celebration of an Indian wedding, but also the intimate, unseen moments that can so easily get lost in the excitement and beautiful chaos of the day.

Because your wedding is more than just a collection of photographs. It is a story; YOUR STORY.

We want our photographs to take you back to how you felt in that moment: the excitement, the laughter, the happy tears, the nervousness, the celebrations, and all the little moments in between.

Years from now, when the flowers have faded and the celebrations have become memories, we hope our photographs give you the chance to relive your wedding day in all its glory, exactly as it happened, and exactly as it felt.

We don’t just photograph weddings. We preserve the feeling of them.
